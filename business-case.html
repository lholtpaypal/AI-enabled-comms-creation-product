Business case for VP review

Comms creation is launch capacity. Launch capacity is TPV capacity.

The current push-campaign path turns a business idea into a cross-functional implementation project. An AI-native creation flow turns the same idea into a structured campaign artifact: copy, audience, creative, experiment, compliance, and system configuration.

2-3 wks Current elapsed time for a standard cross-functional campaign path.
8+ Common handoffs across PM, CommsHub, design, engineering, legal, and ELMO.
<1 day Target common path when agents convert intent into configured campaign inputs.
"There's filing taxes in the United States, then there's creating comms at PayPal."
Jordan Digby testimonial

This is not a tooling complaint. It is a throughput constraint on customer engagement, experimentation velocity, and the number of PayPal moments teams can put into market.

What Actually Changes

The old workflow asks humans to learn internal systems one campaign at a time. The new workflow teaches the systems to agents once, then lets teams express business intent.

Today: human-facing process

Creators spend most of their time translating, waiting, and chasing dependencies.

  1. Campaign creator has an idea, then schedules kickoff and follow-up with CommsHub.
  2. Creator is routed to documentation and requisitions design support.
  3. Design bottleneck forms; borrowed designers onboard to Figma resources and principles.
  4. Event-based personalization creates a new engineering workflow with two engineering teams.
  5. Creator is hand-held through opaque campaign configuration fields and database inputs.
  6. CommsHub engineering audits one-off fields, then ELMO and legal are configured manually.
Target: agent-facing workflow

The PM states intent once; agents produce the campaign package for review.

  1. Creator describes the campaign goal, audience hypothesis, timing, and desired user action.
  2. Agents generate compliant copy, creative specs, deep links, RPS audience options, and event logic.
  3. Experimentation variants and holdout are generated by default, then mapped to ELMO fields.
  4. PIE and database configuration are written from structured outputs instead of manual forms.
  5. Human review focuses on strategy, edge-case legal judgment, and launch approval.
Ease of use Natural-language intent replaces tool fluency as the entry requirement.
More launches Soft-good ideas become cheap experiments instead of abandoned coordination projects.
Better targeting Event-based personalization is preserved instead of dropped to make the process survivable.
Built-in testing Every campaign can ship with variants, holdout, and readable measurement setup.

Why This Matters To PayPal's Enterprise Metrics

PayPal's business is volume-tethered. Faster, more targeted communications increase the number of chances to move customers toward actions that create payment volume. The stock-price story follows the same logic: durable TPV growth, transaction revenue, and engagement are the metrics investors need to believe can keep compounding.

$1.79T FY2025 total payment volume processed by PayPal, up 7% year over year.
$29.8B FY2025 transaction revenue; the company says transaction revenue growth is typically correlated with TPV and payment transactions.
439M Active accounts across approximately 200 markets; communications are a lever for engagement and activation.
Sensitivity: $1M incremental TPV x 1.66% FY2025 transaction-revenue yield = ~$16.6K transaction revenue 1.66% is transaction revenue divided by TPV: $29.798B / $1.79T. Actual campaign economics vary by product mix, funding mix, cross-border share, incentives, losses, and merchant pricing.

Operating implication

When campaign creation is slow, teams ration ideas. They remove personalization, skip variants, delay launches, or stop coming back. That limits the number of tested customer moments PayPal can put into market.

Investor implication

PayPal reports TPV, payment transactions, active accounts, and payment transactions per active account as key metrics that help investors evaluate the business. A faster comms engine is a practical operating lever against those metrics.

Executive Decision

The decision is not whether agents can write copy. The decision is whether PayPal wants comms creation to remain bespoke internal consulting or become scalable commercial infrastructure.

Fund the system context

Prioritize MCP/API access for RPS metadata, PIE fields, ELMO schemas, legal rules, deep links, and event triggers. RPS is the highest-value dependency because audience quality drives relevance.

Standardize the campaign package

Require every campaign to produce the same machine-readable bundle: copy, variants, audience rationale, experiment plan, compliance status, destination, timing, and rollback criteria.

Measure the business loop

Track cycle time, launch conversion, variants per campaign, readout rate, incremental TPV, transaction revenue, opt-outs, and repeat creator usage.
