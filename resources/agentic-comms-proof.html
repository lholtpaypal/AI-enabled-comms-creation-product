First principles — a proof

Comms creation doesn't scale by adding people. We can prove it.

A first-principles case, laid out the way you'd lay out a theorem — premises, proposition, proof — for why campaign creation must become an agent-native workflow, and what engineering investment turns that proof into shipped capacity quickly.

Given

Four premises this room already accepts.

Postulate I

A push notification reaches a customer through an otherwise idle moment and surfaces value they'd capture by using PayPal. It is one of our most powerful brand levers — and it is dramatically under-utilized.

Postulate II

The context needed to build a campaign is scattered across the enterprise. More heads means more hops to retrieve it.

Postulate III

Every step of the build is the same motion: a fresh person retrieves context and enters it into a system. Copy, audience, deep link, experiment — the shape repeats.

Postulate IV

Teaching a human is bespoke onboarding repeated per person, per team, per campaign. Building context for an agent is done once, then reused instantly.

To prove

That campaign creation should run as an agent-native workflow — and that funding engineering now converts a non-scaling service bottleneck into reusable launch capacity.

Proof

Each line follows from the one before it.

Read down the left for the claim, down the right for why it holds. No step asks you to take anything on faith.

And the three things this room asked to see — built in, not bolted on:

i.Experimentation variants

Variants and a holdout are part of the default package — efficacy is measured from the first send.

ii.Ease-of-use

Plain-English intent in, reviewable launch package out. No config-tool hand-holding, no scavenger hunt for context.

iii.No black box

Every field is editable and the agent's work is shown as it happens — humans stay in control of strategy and edge cases.

* Time and cost figures are estimates, consistent with the attached business case. Demonstrated against the workflow you're about to see live.

Corollary

The plan from here — and the one thing it needs.

  1. Guardrails, evals, benchmarks per task. We measure each agent's efficacy before we trust it — no deployment ahead of the data.

  2. Right-size token spend. Start with the most capable model and work backward to the cheapest one that still clears the bar.

  3. Human in the loop through launch. People review strategy, compliance, and edge cases while agents do the data movement.

  4. At threshold, bulk-create — and flip pull to push. Use data to find markets under-using these levers, build the package, and bring it to the team for one-click approve or edit.

  5. Layer Sam's intelligence models. Personalize further and prune low-performing packages in favor of what works.

The ask

Dedicated engineering capacity, now.

The proof holds independent of staffing. What resourcing changes is speed — how fast we cross the benchmark thresholds and start converting the bottleneck into shipped campaigns. Every week unfunded is launch capacity we leave on the table.

Q.E.D. — now, the demonstration.
